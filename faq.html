Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, payments, and your RC5 Elite Motion experience.

How do I book an appointment? +
Click the "Book Your Seat" button on our website. Fill in your name, mobile number, age, gender, focus area, preferred date, and time slot. Once submitted, your seat will be reserved and you'll be prompted to complete the payment to confirm your booking.
How do I make payment? +
After reserving your seat, you'll be directed to our secure Razorpay payment gateway. We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and popular wallets. The workshop fee is ₹249 (discounted from ₹1500). Payment is required to confirm your booking.
Can I cancel my appointment? +
Cancellations can be requested by contacting us via WhatsApp or phone at least 24 hours before your scheduled appointment. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the session may not be eligible for a refund. Please refer to our Terms & Conditions for the full cancellation policy.
Can I reschedule my appointment? +
Yes, rescheduling is allowed subject to slot availability. Please contact us via WhatsApp at least 24 hours before your appointment to request a reschedule. We'll do our best to accommodate your preferred new date and time.
What are the clinic timings? +
We are open Monday to Saturday. Morning sessions run from 7:00 AM to 1:00 PM, followed by a lunch break from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM, and evening sessions from 2:00 PM to 9:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays.
Where is the clinic located? +
RC5 Elite Motion is located at 123, Wellness Avenue, Sector 14, Mumbai, Maharashtra — 400001. You can find us on Google Maps by searching "RC5 Elite Motion". For directions, feel free to WhatsApp us and we'll guide you.
How do I contact the clinic? +
You can reach us by phone at +91 98765 43210, via WhatsApp on the same number, or by email at hello@rc5elitemotion.com. You can also use the contact form on our Contact Us page. We typically respond within a few hours during clinic hours.
Do I receive a WhatsApp confirmation? +
Yes! Once your payment is confirmed, our team will send you a WhatsApp message with your booking details, session information, and any preparation instructions. Please ensure your mobile number is correct when booking.
Can I pay using UPI? +
Absolutely! We accept all major UPI apps including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and any UPI-enabled banking app. Simply select UPI on the Razorpay payment screen and complete the payment using your preferred app.
What should I bring for my appointment? +
Please wear comfortable, stretchable clothing that allows free movement. Bring your booking confirmation (Booking ID), a water bottle, and any previous medical reports or prescriptions if relevant to your condition. Arrive 5–10 minutes early to complete a brief intake form.
