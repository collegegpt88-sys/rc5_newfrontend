Our Story

RC5 Elite Motion was founded with a single belief — that every person deserves to move freely, feel strong, and live without pain. What started as a small movement coaching practice has grown into a premium wellness workshop trusted by thousands across India.

Our founder, Devang Kacha, spent years studying movement science, rehabilitation, and performance coaching before creating the RC5 methodology — a holistic approach that addresses the root cause of movement dysfunction rather than just the symptoms.

Today, RC5 Elite Motion is home to a team of world-class coaches who are passionate about transforming lives through the power of movement.
