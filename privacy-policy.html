🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

Last updated: January 2026 — RC5 Elite Motion is committed to your privacy.

1 Information We Collect

When you use our website or book an appointment, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal details: name, age, gender
  • Contact information: mobile number, email address
  • Appointment details: preferred date, time, and focus area
  • Health information voluntarily provided during the booking or session
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, and pages visited (via cookies)

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and confirm your appointment bookings
  • Send booking confirmations and session reminders via WhatsApp
  • Personalise your coaching and wellness experience
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3 Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us understand how you use our website.

  • Essential cookies: required for the website to function properly
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand visitor behaviour (anonymised)
  • Preference cookies: remember your settings and preferences

You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

4 Payment Security

All payments are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. RC5 Elite Motion does not store, process, or have access to your full payment card details.

  • All payment transactions are encrypted using SSL/TLS technology
  • Razorpay's security practices comply with international payment security standards
  • We only receive a transaction confirmation and masked payment details

5 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Razorpay — for secure payment processing
  • WhatsApp Business — for booking confirmations and communication
  • Google Fonts — for website typography (no personal data shared)
  • Google Maps — for displaying our clinic location

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

6 Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • Data is stored on secure servers with restricted access
  • We retain your data only for as long as necessary to provide our services
  • You may request deletion of your personal data by contacting us
  • In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users as required by law

7 Patient Privacy

We treat all health-related information shared with us with the utmost confidentiality.

  • Health information is used solely to personalise your coaching experience
  • We do not share your health information with any third party without your consent
  • Our coaches are bound by professional confidentiality obligations
  • You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your health data
