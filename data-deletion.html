Data Deletion Instructions

How to request permanent deletion of the personal data RC5 Elite Motion has collected about you.

Last updated: January 2026

1 What Data We Store

When you book a workshop seat or a consultation, we store the following information linked to your booking:

  • Name, mobile number, email address, age, and gender
  • Appointment/consultation details (date, time, focus area)
  • Payment reference details from Razorpay (no card details are stored by us)
  • WhatsApp messages sent to you for booking confirmations and reminders

2 How to Request Deletion

You can request permanent deletion of your personal data at any time, in any of the following ways:

  • WhatsApp: Message us at +91 97143 51278 with the text "DELETE MY DATA" along with your registered mobile number or Booking ID
  • Email: Send a request to hello@rc5elitemotion.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request", including your registered mobile number or Booking ID

Please allow us to verify your identity using the mobile number or Booking ID linked to your data before we proceed.

3 What Happens Next

  • We confirm receipt of your request within 48 hours
  • Your personal data (name, mobile, email, booking records) is permanently deleted from our database within 7 business days
  • Data required by law (e.g. payment records for tax/accounting purposes) may be retained only as long as legally required, then deleted
  • We will send you a final confirmation once deletion is complete

4 Contact Us

For any questions about this process, reach out to:

  • Email: hello@rc5elitemotion.com
  • Phone / WhatsApp: +91 97143 51278

See also our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your data.
